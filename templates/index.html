System Architecture & Methodology

1. Why Chained Classifiers?

No single authoritative dataset simultaneously labels text for both sarcasm and hate speech. Attempting to manually label a joint corpus from scratch was prohibitive. Therefore, this project chains two domain-specialized models: a contextual toxicity classifier and an irony/sarcasm classifier.

2. Datasets Used Across Iterations

Hate & Offensive (English) Davidson et al. (2017) + Implicit Hate Corpus DistilBERT Contextual Transformer
English Sarcasm Reddit SARC (Khodak et al.) 100k comments TF-IDF (1-2 ngrams) + LinearSVC
Hinglish Sarcasm Human-Annotated Hinglish Tweets Char n-gram (2-4) + LinearSVC
V7 Combined Research Davidson + HateXplain (Cleaned cross-split) Latest benchmark ready for fine-tuning

3. Known Limitations & Ethical Safeguards

  • Devanagari Label Leakage: An earlier Devanagari Hindi dataset exhibited severe collection leakage (topic clustering and hashtags baked into text). Rather than deploying an unreliable model, Devanagari inputs are explicitly flagged and rejected.
  • Language Heuristics: Script routing between English and Hinglish uses lexical marker overlap rather than an oversized language-ID dependency.
  • Cross-Domain Nuance: Hate speech is evaluated contextually via transformer subwords, while sarcasm uses dense lexical features.
